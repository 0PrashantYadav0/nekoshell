# greet

## What it does

Prints a greeting when you open a terminal: a random Pokémon sprite, or a
picture from your own art pack, next to the machine stats
[fastfetch](https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch) collects — OS, host,
uptime, shell, CPU, memory, disk, battery, wifi, packages.

Which one you get is a roll: `POKEMON_SHARE` in `~/.config/nekoshell/greet.conf`
is the percentage that goes to the Pokémon (70 by default), and 1 in
`SHINY_ODDS` Pokémon is shiny. The picture half only happens in a terminal
that can draw inline images — the terminal adapter is what says so, not the
`TERM_PROGRAM` variable — and falls back to a Pokémon everywhere else.

`nekoshell greet` prints it on demand, `--image` and `--text` force a branch,
and `nekoshell art` manages the pack. The greeting stays out of the way: it
says nothing inside tmux, inside a panel, over SSH (unless
`NEKOSHELL_GREET_SSH=1`), when stdout is not a terminal, or under Claude Code.

## Installs

`fastfetch` (Homebrew formula), and
[pokemon-colorscripts](https://gitlab.com/phoneybadger/pokemon-colorscripts),
which is not in Homebrew: it is checked out at a pinned commit into
`~/.local/share/pokemon-colorscripts` and symlinked onto your PATH at
`~/.local/bin/pokemon-colorscripts`.

## Files

Copied into your home, once, and yours from then on:

- `~/.config/nekoshell/greet.conf` — the share, the shiny odds, the image size

Generated by nekoshell and rewritten on every theme switch:

- `~/.config/fastfetch/config.jsonc` — from `fastfetch.jsonc.tmpl` beside this
  README, with the flavour's colours substituted. Edit the template, not the
  rendered file.

Your own:

- `~/.config/nekoshell/art/` — the art pack. `nekoshell art sample` copies the
  three shipped samples in, `nekoshell art add <image>` adds your own.

nekoshell ships no anime stills and no Pokémon sprites: those are copyrighted.
The samples are the project's own pixel art, and the Pokémon come from
pokemon-colorscripts at greeting time.

## After install

Nothing. Open a new terminal and the greeting is there.
`nekoshell art sample` if you want the shipped pictures in rotation.

## Remove

`nekoshell plugin remove greet` stops the greeting and takes `nekoshell greet`
and `nekoshell art` away with it. Your `greet.conf` and your art pack stay
where they are — they are yours — as does the pokemon-colorscripts checkout.
Add `--purge` to uninstall fastfetch too, as long as no other enabled plugin
needs it.
