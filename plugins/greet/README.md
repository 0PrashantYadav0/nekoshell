# greet

## What it does

Prints a greeting when you open a terminal: a sprite from an enabled art
provider plugin (a Pokémon, an anime character, a Minecraft block, an ANSI
pattern), or a picture from your own art pack, next to the machine stats
[fastfetch](https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch) collects — OS, host,
uptime, shell, CPU, memory, disk, battery, wifi, packages.

An art provider is a plugin that ships a `greet-art` program: `pokemon` (in
every profile), `anime`, `minecraft` and `colorscripts`. `ART` in
`~/.config/nekoshell/greet.conf` picks among the enabled ones: `auto` gives
each the same odds, `pokemon:70,anime:30` weights them, and a name that is
not enabled is skipped. `SPRITE_SHARE` is the percentage of launches that
show a sprite rather than a picture from the art pack (70 by default); the
picture half only happens in a terminal that can draw inline images — the
terminal adapter is what says so, not the `TERM_PROGRAM` variable — and
falls back to a sprite everywhere else. With no provider enabled the stats
print on their own.

`nekoshell greet` prints it on demand, `--image`, `--text` and `--art NAME`
force a branch, and `nekoshell art` manages the pack. The greeting stays out
of the way: it says nothing inside tmux, inside a panel, over SSH (unless
`NEKOSHELL_GREET_SSH=1`), when stdout is not a terminal, or under Claude Code.

## Installs

`fastfetch` (Homebrew formula). The sprites come from the provider plugins,
each of which fetches its own pack.

## Files

Copied into your home, once, and yours from then on:

- `~/.config/nekoshell/greet.conf` — `ART`, `SPRITE_SHARE`, the image size,
  and any provider's own keys (`POKEMON_SHINY_ODDS`, `ANIME_ONLY`, ...)

Generated by nekoshell and rewritten on every theme switch:

- `~/.config/fastfetch/config.jsonc` — from `fastfetch.jsonc.tmpl` beside this
  README, with the flavour's colours substituted. Edit the template, not the
  rendered file.

Your own:

- `~/.config/nekoshell/art/` — the art pack. `nekoshell art sample` copies the
  three shipped samples in, `nekoshell art add <image>` adds your own.

nekoshell ships no anime stills and no Pokémon sprites: those are copyrighted.
The samples are the project's own pixel art, and the sprites come from the
provider plugins' packs at greeting time.

## After install

Enable an art provider: `nekoshell plugin add pokemon` (every profile does
this for you); `anime`, `minecraft` and `colorscripts` are one `plugin add`
away too. `nekoshell art sample` if you want the shipped pictures in
rotation. The doctor warns until a provider is enabled.

## Remove

`nekoshell plugin remove greet` stops the greeting and takes `nekoshell greet`
and `nekoshell art` away with it (the providers have to go first: they need
greet). Your `greet.conf` and your art pack stay where they are — they are
yours. Add `--purge` to uninstall fastfetch too, as long as no other enabled
plugin needs it.
