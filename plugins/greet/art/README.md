# Art pack samples

`neko.png`, `ghost.png` and `slime.png` are original pixel art generated by `plugins/greet/scripts/gen-sample-art.py`. They are MIT licensed like the rest of the repo.

nekoshell does not ship anime stills, Pokémon sprites or Minecraft textures: those are copyrighted. The sprites come from the art provider plugins' packs (pokemon-colorscripts, anime-colorscripts, minecraft-colorscripts) at greeting time. To use your own images:

    nekoshell art add ~/Pictures/my-favourite.png
    nekoshell art list

Images live in `~/.config/nekoshell/art/`. PNG and JPG work. Transparent PNGs look best. Size is set in `~/.config/nekoshell/greet.conf` (`IMAGE_WIDTH`, `IMAGE_HEIGHT`, in terminal cells).
